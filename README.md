# Nalu Ledger Forensics

**Nalu Ledger Forensics** explores what actually happened on the XRP Ledger and how ledger activity should be interpreted in context.

Rather than treating every large transfer, high transaction count, or unusual pattern as suspicious, the platform is designed around **evidence, account role, historical context, data quality, and confidence**.

Areas of investigation include:

- Complete XRPL account-history reconstruction
- Account creation and lifecycle verification
- Balance and asset-change reconstruction
- XRP and issued-asset flows
- Counterparty relationships
- Fan-in / fan-out activity
- Account control state
- Regular keys and signer lists
- Issuer and treasury behavior
- Token distribution
- DEX participation
- AMM and LP behavior
- Market-integrity signals
- Wash-trading candidates
- Spoofing / layering candidates
- Liquidity changes
- Security events
- Evidence-backed findings
- Historical timelines
- Relationship graphs
- Raw ledger provenance

A major principle behind Nalu is:

> **An anomaly is not automatically wrongdoing. Evidence, context, role, history, and alternative explanations matter.**

# Nalu Ledger Crawler

**Nalu Ledger Crawler** expands the investigation model beyond individual wallets.

Instead of asking only:

> “What happened to this account?”

the crawler can investigate:

> “What happened across this market, token, liquidity pool, issuer, or network of related activity?”

The crawler works through validated XRPL history and progressively builds reusable knowledge from it.

Current and planned investigation modes include:

### Account Crawl
Reconstruct an account from its verified creation or earliest available history through the latest validated ledger.

### Token Crawl
Explore:

- Issuance
- Trustlines
- Holder distribution
- Early holders
- Transfers
- DEX activity
- AMM participation
- Liquidity
- Large sellers
- Distribution changes

### Follow Money
Trace economic value through:

- Direct XRP transfers
- Issued assets
- DEX executions
- AMM swaps
- Liquidity events
- Counterparties
- Multi-step economic paths

while preserving the difference between **directly traceable value** and **probabilistic economic continuation**.

### Market Crawl
Reconstruct:

- Offer lifecycles
- Order-book behavior
- Actual executions
- Cancellations
- Fill rates
- Market participants
- Market regimes
- AMM activity
- Executable liquidity
- Order-book / AMM interactions

### Liquidity Crawl
Investigate:

- LP deposits
- LP withdrawals
- LP concentration
- Pool-depth changes
- Auction slots
- Fee voting
- Liquidity shocks
- Large participant behavior

### Setup Crawl
Reconstruct what occurred **before, during, and after** important market events.

Examples include:

- Liquidity added before a volume increase
- Large holder selling
- Order walls appearing and disappearing
- Liquidity withdrawal after market activity
- Repeated market sequences

The goal is not to automatically accuse participants of manipulation.

The goal is to **reconstruct the setup, identify patterns, compare them against normal behavior, surface supporting and reducing evidence, and allow the user to inspect the ledger proof directly**.
